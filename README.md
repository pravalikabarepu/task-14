# Northwind SQL Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Northwind database using SQL to generate useful business insights related to sales, products, categories, employees, and suppliers.

The analysis uses SQL queries, JOINs, aggregation functions, Common Table Expressions (CTEs), window functions, filtering, sorting, and calculations to understand sales performance.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify top-selling products
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Compare sales across categories
- Evaluate employee sales performance
- Analyze supplier performance
- Identify products with no sales
- Understand product and category distribution

## Tools Used

## Queries and Findings

### Query 1 — Overall Sales Analysis
Calculates overall sales using quantity, unit price, and discount.

**Finding:** Provides an overall view of sales generated from the orders data.

### Query 2 — Top 10 Products by Sales
Identifies the products generating the highest sales.

**Finding:** Highlights the best-performing products based on total sales.

### Query 3 — Monthly Sales Trend
Calculates monthly sales and month-over-month growth.

**Finding:** Shows monthly sales performance and changes in sales compared with the previous month.

### Query 4 — Sales by Category
Calculates total sales for each product category.

**Finding:** Identifies the categories generating the highest sales.

### Query 5 — Sales by Employee
Calculates total sales handled by each employee.

**Finding:** Compares employee sales performance and identifies high-performing employees.

### Query 6 — Sales by Product
Calculates total sales generated by each product.

**Finding:** Identifies products contributing the most to total sales.

### Query 7 — Quantity Sold by Product
Calculates the total quantity sold for each product.

**Finding:** Identifies products with the highest sales volume.

### Query 8 — Products by Category
Counts the number of products available in each category.

**Finding:** Shows which categories have the largest product range.

### Query 9 — Sales by Supplier
Calculates sales associated with each supplier's products.

**Finding:** Identifies suppliers whose products generate the highest sales.

### Query 10 — Orders by Employee
Counts the number of orders handled by each employee.

**Finding:** Compares employee workload based on the number of orders handled.

### Query 11 — Products with No Sales
Identifies products that do not have any matching orders.

**Finding:** Highlights products that may require additional business attention.

### Query 12 — Category Performance
Compares product count and total sales by category.

**Finding:** Helps identify strong and weak categories by comparing product availability with sales performance.

### Query 13 — Top 10 Products by Sales
Returns the 10 products with the highest total sales.

**Finding:** Provides a focused view of the most valuable products.

### Query 14 — Top 10 Employees by Sales
Returns the top employees based on total sales.

**Finding:** Highlights the strongest employee contributors to sales.

### Query 15 — Supplier Product Count
Counts the number of products supplied by each supplier.

**Finding:** Identifies suppliers with the largest product ranges.
